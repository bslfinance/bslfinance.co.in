BSL Nidhi LimitedRecurring Deposit Scheme

Build your wealth through disciplined regular savings

Overview & Highlights

Are you looking for an investment opportunity where you can make regular monthly contributions and earn handsome returns? A Recurring Deposit (RD) is tailored to help you save systematically.

Deposit a fixed installment each month for a chosen duration and earn compound interest calculated on a quarterly basis. It encourages continuous thrift while steadily building a corpus for your future.

Terms & Conditions
  • Membership in BSL Nidhi Limited and valid KYC documentation are required to open an RD account.
  • Flexible tenure options ranging from 1 year to 5 years.
  • Interest is calculated on a quarterly compounded basis.
  • Maturity amount will be paid along with accumulated interest upon surrendering passbook and signed maturity application.
  • Loan facility is available against active, regular Recurring Deposits.
