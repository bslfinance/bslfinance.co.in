BSL Nidhi LimitedCompany Profile

Previous Next

BSL Nidhi Limited is a Nidhi company incorporated under the Companies Act 2013 & regulated by Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India. The main objective of company is to cultivate the habit of thrift and savings among its members & receiving deposits from, and lending to, members only, for their mutual benefit.

The Company has started its business with great enthusiasm and dedication which crafts the company into one of the leading Nidhi Companies in India. We are committed to the financial empowerment of our members by delivering services in the most transparent, ethical, and efficient manner.

We follow transparent, fair, ethical and legally tenable practices while conducting business. Our products are designed to deliver maximum value, offering attractive rates of interest along with other member benefits.

Our Mission

Our mission is to offer high-quality financial services which meet the evolving needs of our members and service areas; and to earn the enduring trust and confidence of all stakeholders, exceeding their expectations and establishing the Company as a premier financial choice.

Our Vision

BSL Nidhi Limited is determined to accomplish its vision of becoming a major regional financial institution. The company's strategic framework is built upon four timeless pillars: integrity & honesty, meaningful relationships, dedicated hard work, and dynamic adaptability.
